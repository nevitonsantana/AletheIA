AletheIA documents different kinds of proof. They should not be treated as interchangeable.

<Badge variant="success">Field evidence is labeled</Badge> <Badge>Examples are not proof</Badge> <Badge variant="accent">Guidance remains canonical</Badge>

## How evidence is classified

| Type | What it is | What it can support | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical guidance | Contracts, ADRs, policies, and accepted definitions | Intended rules, boundaries, and vocabulary | That a rule worked in a real project |
| Field evidence | A source-backed real pilot, adoption record, or Work Slice closeout | What happened in that bounded context | Universal performance or a reusable control by itself |
| Synthetic example | An illustrative template, fixture, mock record, or walkthrough | How to use a structure or read an artifact | Real adoption, measured benefit, or enforcement |
| Historical experiment | A preserved trial or superseded path | Context for past decisions and learning | Current support or active direction |

## Closeouts and operation records

Closeouts capture what happened in a bounded operation: intent, scope, tools, evidence, decisions, remaining risk, and the next safe step. They are useful evidence when their source references and limits are clear.

Browse [operation closeouts](/pilots/closeouts/README/) for preserved records. Treat them as contextual evidence, not an instruction manual or a generic performance benchmark.

## How to use a case responsibly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the label">
    Confirm whether you are reading guidance, field evidence, a synthetic example, or historical material.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the boundary">
    Identify project context, scope, evidence source, missing data, and explicit stop conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer only what is justified">
    Reuse a pattern or question when it is portable. Do not carry over product-specific assumptions, synthetic numbers, or unstated permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your own evidence">
    Apply guidance in a real bounded Work Slice and record the result before treating it as local proof.
  </Step>
</Steps>
