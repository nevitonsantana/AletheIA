Boundary

  • APOB is represented as an input/source layer only.
  • Signals show availability and provenance; they do not create governance authority.
  • `unavailable` is neutral and must not be colored as failure.
  • Estimated values must stay labeled and cannot replace reported source records.

Not included

  • No telemetry collection.
  • No cost calculation.
  • No backend, runtime, collector, schema, or policy engine.
  • No Adaptive Skills integration.
