- Treat AletheIA as the macro layer for framing, governance, decision, validation, handoff, and continuity.
- Keep project-local rules and human accountability explicit; installation does not grant permissions or create a security boundary.
- Keep Adaptive Skills separate: they are complementary microcapabilities, not a replacement for AletheIA governance.
